Meet the people behind the play

About Niko's Games

Niko's Games began with one little boy named Niko and a simple idea: create a welcoming place where children can play, explore, and practice new skills at their own pace.

Our story

Inspired by Niko, a young boy whose curiosity sparked an idea, we set out to create educational games where children ages 3–9 can learn, explore, and have fun at their own pace. We believe every child deserves a welcoming place to play and discover, with thoughtful attention to simplicity, accessibility, and sensory-friendly experiences.

Our work brings together hands-on technology and creative development with real-world experience supporting children in classrooms. We are building Niko's Games one thoughtful adventure at a time.

Our founding team

Yovanny Alfonso

Founder & Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Yovanny is the creator of Niko's Games. With 23 years of experience in technology, including software development and marketing, he leads the technical architecture, software development, and product innovation. Inspired by Niko, he wanted to create something children and families could enjoy together.

Laury Alfonso

Co-Founder & Educational Advisor

Laury brings 17 years of experience supporting students from kindergarten through grade 12, including special education. She helps bring a caring educational perspective to Niko's Games and provides the friendly recorded voice children hear as they play.

Built with families in mind

We aim for simple instructions, approachable challenges, clear choices, and sensory-considerate settings. Every child learns differently, and we want families to feel welcome exploring together.
