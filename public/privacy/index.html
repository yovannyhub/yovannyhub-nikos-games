Privacy Policy

Effective date: Pending final review and publication.

Pre-launch draft: Final review of hosting, authentication, payments, analytics, and children's data practices is required before paid launch.

Niko's Games is operated by Calm Smiles LLC. Contact: support@playnikogames.com.

Who uses the service

Niko's Games offers educational games for children ages 3–9. Adults are responsible for subscriptions and purchases. We aim to minimize collection of personal information from children.

Game progress and settings

The game currently stores progress, stars, scores, and preferences in your browser's local storage. This information may remain on that device until cleared. Any optional player-name feature must be reviewed before launch to determine whether it collects personal information.

Payments and subscriptions

When a parent purchases a subscription, payment and subscription information is handled by our payment and subscription service providers, Stripe and RevenueCat. We do not intend to collect payment-card details directly in the game. The providers' own privacy notices may apply.

Technical information

Hosting, security, and service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the website. We will confirm our specific retention and sharing practices before publication.

Children's privacy

We do not intend to use children's personal information for behavioral advertising. Before introducing accounts, analytics, or other features that collect personal information from children under 13, we will assess applicable parental notice and consent obligations, including COPPA.

Your choices

Parents may contact us to ask about information we hold, correction, deletion, and subscription privacy matters. Clearing browser storage can remove locally saved game progress but does not cancel a paid subscription.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes and will publish the effective date of the final policy. Questions: support@playnikogames.com.
