Refund Policy

Pre-launch draft: This page is being prepared. Final contact details and policies will be confirmed before paid subscriptions launch.

Niko's Games Premium costs US $27 per year and provides access to Worlds 2–10. World 1 is free.

Refund requests

Parents may request a refund if they believe a charge was made in error or they are dissatisfied with their purchase. Our proposed 7-day first-purchase refund guarantee is not yet finalized; we will publish the confirmed eligibility rules before enabling public checkout.

How to request help

Visit our Support page for contact information once the dedicated support mailbox is activated. Please keep your receipt and provide the purchase email and transaction date. Never send your full card number.

Cancellation versus refund

Canceling a subscription stops future renewals but does not automatically refund a completed payment. Unless a refund changes access, canceled memberships remain usable through the paid period. Applicable consumer rights are not restricted.
