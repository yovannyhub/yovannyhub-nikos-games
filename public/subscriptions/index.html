Subscriptions & Cancellation

Pre-launch draft: Review these subscription disclosures and the support contact before enabling public paid checkout.

Free and premium access

World 1 is free. Niko's Games Premium unlocks Worlds 2–10 for US $27 per year, plus any applicable taxes shown at checkout.

Automatic renewal

The annual subscription renews automatically until canceled. The renewal price and terms are disclosed at checkout.

How to cancel

A parent or guardian can manage an active subscription through the Parent Account & Membership area on the game homepage, or request assistance by emailing support@playnikogames.com using the email associated with the purchase. The parent account provides a secure subscription-management link when available. Do not send full payment-card numbers by email.

Cancel before the next renewal to avoid the next recurring charge. Cancellation generally stops future renewals while access continues through the paid term, subject to applicable law and the terms shown at checkout.

Refund requests

For refund requests, email support@playnikogames.com with your purchase email and date. Requests will be reviewed according to applicable law and the terms presented at checkout.

Important

Deleting the game, clearing browser data, or stopping play does not cancel a subscription. Parents should keep purchase receipts for account recovery.
