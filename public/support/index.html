Membership help

From the Niko's Games homepage, choose Grown-ups, then Parent account & membership. Sign in with the email used at purchase to check your subscription. When available, select Manage or cancel subscription to open the secure billing portal.

When contacting support

Include your parent account email, approximate purchase date, and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, magic sign-in links, or full payment-card numbers.
